Tebak Lagu Online Gratis

GUESS SONG adalah game tebak lagu di browser: dengarkan potongan audio mulai dari 0,1 detik, lalu tebak judul atau nama artisnya. Pilih katalog lagu Indonesia atau internasional, genre (pop, rock, elektronik, hip-hop, R&B, alternative, Latin), dan tahun rilis dari sebelum 2000 sampai sekarang. Naik level dari Easy, Medium, Hard, Impossible sampai Music God yang berisi lagu-lagu yang jarang diketahui orang.

How to play

  1. Choose catalog (All, Indonesian, or International), genre, and release year range.
  2. Press Start to get a random curated song. Selections are locked during each round.
  3. Listen to the audio clip and guess the song title or artist name.
  4. Each wrong guess or skip unlocks longer clips: 0.1s → 1s → 3s → 5s → 10s.
  5. Guess correctly to advance levels: Easy → Medium → Hard → Impossible → Music God.
  6. If you run out of attempts or forfeit, the streak is lost and you reset back to Easy!

Release year refers to initial song release. Difficulty scales by Deezer popularity rank. 30-second audio previews are streamed directly from Deezer.
